# Editorial direction

The site should make Alex's judgment visible through work, reasoning, and choices. It should read as a personal site with serious work behind it. The desk, Oliver, music, and personal experiments belong here.

## The central thesis

As intelligence becomes cheaper and more abundant, prediction, software, and information become less exclusive. Economic power increasingly depends on converting intelligence into consequential outcomes through capital, physical infrastructure, data, permissions, distribution, and institutions.

The questions are who controls those conversion mechanisms, who captures the value, who bears the risks, and whether individuals retain meaningful agency. The entrepreneurial ambition is to build useful systems at those points. The personal ambition is ownership, competence, and financial independence without abandoning the principles that make freedom worth having.

Sovereignty means the capacity to determine one's outcomes, understand and change the systems one depends on, and retain meaningful choices when circumstances change. Reduction is the method: identify the few constraints that determine what can happen. These ideas connect the professional, business, and personal work without requiring them to form a tidy identity.

## How that appears on the site

- Home introduces the person, current question, and actual work. It should leave room for curiosity.
- Work shows a decision, implementation, and outcome. The operations record supplies context and evidence; the resume holds the full chronology.
- Research distinguishes an argument from a measurement. Keep announced, contracted, financed, energized, billable, and cash-producing capacity separate. No claim of investment alpha without a tested result.
- Projects explain a user problem, a concrete design choice, present status, and useful evidence. A signed artifact proves the recorded bytes; it does not establish truth or product effectiveness.
- Writing preserves original thinking, experiences, and tension. Bound analogies. Remove unsupported factual claims. Never invent private motives or manufacture confessional stories to make the author seem human.
- About can state the ambition directly and acknowledge contradictions. The site does not need to advertise every current business investigation.

## Editorial checks

Ask what a skeptical stranger can actually learn or inspect. Prefer a specific decision to an identity slogan. Keep commercial ambition visible without implying customers, returns, expertise, or scale that have not been demonstrated. A limit should explain the evidence, not turn every paragraph into an apology.

An image should document work, explain a decision, or show something personal. Avoid access screens, abstract graphs, and detailed diagrams used as signals of sophistication. Keep screenshots readable and caption examples as examples.

Avoid tags, italic emphasis, Instrument Serif, negative kerning, elaborate UI, dividers, many shades and opacities, bordered containers, decorative diagrams, code breadcrumbs, and section numbering. Use space, readable type, and alignment. The playful elements already supply personality.

## Source and publication boundaries

The October 2026 revision uses the current working research paper and the October 16 resume as context. The research paper remains a private draft, with AI research and editing assistance and empirical work still to do. Public copy must not imply that the whole draft has been independently validated. The resume remains the source for career dates and operating achievements.

Keep employer names on the resume only. Keep family relationships, private records, customer/account data, personal financial positions, and evaluation-client material off public pages. A new venture is an investigation until its delivery is evidenced.
